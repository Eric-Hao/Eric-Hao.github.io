Following vortex surfaces beyond ideal flow

越过理想流体假设，追踪真正的涡面

A transport velocity is not necessarily the fluid velocity. The distinction improves tracking, but only under explicit existence and approximation conditions.输运涡面的速度不一定等于流体速度；这一差别能改善追踪，却必须受存在性条件与近似误差约束。

1. Elevator pitch核心思想一句话总结

A virtual velocity preserves vortex-surface geometry where its smoothness conditions hold; controlled simulations show why physical-velocity tracking can mislead.

在光滑性条件成立时，虚拟速度可保持涡面；受控模拟揭示真实速度追踪为何会误导。

2. Background & motivation论文背景与动机

The prerequisite distinction

Imagine marking a rotating fluid with dye. A material surface follows the same fluid particles. A vortex surface instead contains the local vorticity direction everywhere: vorticity, ω=∇×u\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf uω=∇×u, measures local fluid rotation, and its integral curves are vortex lines. These are different definitions, not rival ways of detecting every possible “vortex”. A vortex-surface field (VSF) is a scalar label ϕv\phi_vϕv​ whose constant-value surfaces are tangent to vorticity. [§1; §3.1, PDF pp.1–4,10]

必要概念：粒子身份与涡面身份

设想给旋转流体染色。物质面跟随同一批流体粒子；涡面则要求处处包含局部涡量方向。涡量 ω=∇×u\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf uω=∇×u 描述流体局部旋转，其积分曲线是涡线。这是两种定义，不是对所有“旋涡”的两套识别标准。涡面场（VSF）是标量标签 ϕv\phi_vϕv​，它的等值面与涡量相切。[§1；§3.1，PDF 第1–4、10页]

Circulation is the line integral of velocity around a loop; by Stokes’ theorem it equals vorticity flux through a spanning surface. In ideal, inviscid, barotropic flow with conservative forces, circulation conservation makes material transport preserve vortex geometry. Viscosity, pressure–density misalignment and non-conservative forces break that implication. The paper does not erase those forces: it asks whether a different carrier velocity can absorb their effect on vorticity transport. [§2.1–2.2, PDF pp.4–6]

Before this work, virtual-velocity formulations existed, but global smooth existence was not guaranteed. The two-time VSF algorithm could enforce tangency numerically, yet still drift between different valid surfaces. The novelty is a conditional transport formulation plus concrete exact and approximate tests, rather than the invention of virtual velocity itself. [§1, PDF pp.2–3]

环量是速度沿闭合回路的线积分；由 Stokes 定理，它等于穿过回路所围曲面的涡量通量。对于无黏、正压且仅受保守力作用的理想流动，环量守恒使物质输运保持涡面几何。黏性、压力与密度梯度不对齐以及非保守力会破坏这一推论。论文没有消除这些力，而是询问：能否用另一输运速度吸收它们对涡量演化的影响？[§2.1–2.2，PDF 第4–6页]

已有研究提出过虚拟速度，但不能保证全局光滑解存在。原有双时间 VSF 算法能数值满足相切约束，却仍可能在不同合法涡面之间漂移。本文新意是有条件的输运表述与具体的精确、近似测试，而非首次提出虚拟速度。[§1，PDF 第2–3页]

3. The core method核心方法 / 模型详解

From force to carrier velocity

Write all non-ideal acceleration terms as F\mathbf FF. Taking the curl removes conservative gradients. The goal is to choose a velocity v\mathbf vv that reproduces the same vorticity evolution without an explicit source term. Here ∇×\nabla\times∇× is curl, and ttt is time. [Eqs.(2.4),(2.7)–(2.8), PDF pp.4–5]

A sufficient construction is ∇×F∥=0\nabla\times\mathbf F_\parallel=0∇×F∥​=0 with nonzero vorticity throughout the region swept by the tracked object, plus a compatible smooth potential. In the controlled modified Navier–Stokes (MNS) flow, the authors remove the vorticity-parallel viscous force. This changes the physical equations and reduces enstrophy dissipation; it is an artificial test, not a more accurate real fluid. In the physical MHD case, they instead approximate the carrier while keeping the MHD solution unchanged. Mixing these two interventions would overstate the result. [§2.5; §6.2, PDF pp.9–10,21]

一个充分构造是在追踪对象扫过的区域内满足 ∇×F∥=0\nabla\times\mathbf F_\parallel=0∇×F∥​=0、涡量非零，并存在相容的光滑势函数。令平行漂移为零，得到 v=u+ω×F⊥/∣ω∣2\mathbf v=\mathbf u+\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf F_\perp/|\boldsymbol\omega|^2v=u+ω×F⊥​/∣ω∣2。这些条件是充分而非必要条件，附录 A.2 给出可积流动反例。涡量零点使分式奇异；任意三维流动中的全局存在性与唯一性仍未解决。[§2.4；附录 A.2，PDF 第8、28页]

受控的修正 Navier–Stokes（MNS）流动删除了黏性力沿涡量方向的分量。这改变了物理方程并减弱涡量平方的耗散，是人工测试，而非更准确的真实流体。对于物理 MHD 算例，作者则保留 MHD 解，仅近似输运速度。混淆两种干预会夸大结果。[§2.5；§6.2，PDF 第9–10、21页]

The numerical pipeline starts from a VSF satisfying tangency, predicts its transport, then corrects it along frozen instantaneous vorticity in an auxiliary pseudo-time. Fifth-order WENO spatial differencing supplies dissipative regularization; time integration for the VSF uses third-order Runge–Kutta. The exact-carrier version mainly corrects numerical errors; the physical-carrier version also corrects non-ideal tangency errors. Removing the correction loses tangency; retaining it alone does not guarantee temporal identity. [§4.3, PDF pp.13–14]

5. Contribution & impact论文的贡献与影响

Contribution and next tests

The strongest contribution is separating instantaneous tangency, temporal coherence and genuine topology change. Exact transport in a modified dissipative system provides a controlled reference; approximate transport in an unchanged physical system demonstrates practical diagnostic value. The general existence theorem and general turbulence applicability remain open, and the separate proof supplement was not audited here.

A concrete future test is to vary the null regularization ϵ\epsilonϵ and grid resolution together while measuring flux error before contact; this would distinguish robust transport from regularization-dependent apparent topology. A second is to compare the omitted-gauge approximation against a numerically solved compatible gauge as force–vorticity alignment deteriorates. Neither test should be replaced by selecting prettier surfaces. [§4.4; §7, PDF pp.15,25–26]

贡献与下一步验证

最强贡献是区分瞬时相切、时间一致性与真实拓扑变化。修正耗散系统中的精确输运提供受控参照；不改变物理系统的近似输运展示诊断价值。一般存在性与一般湍流适用性仍开放，本文也未审计独立证明补充材料。

一个具体后续实验是联合改变零点正则化 ϵ\epsilonϵ 与网格分辨率，测量接触前的通量误差，以区分稳健输运与依赖正则化的表观拓扑。另一个实验是在力与涡量垂直关系变差时，把忽略规范项的近似与数值求解相容规范的结果比较。这两项都不能由挑选更好看的曲面替代。[§4.4；§7，PDF 第15、25–26页]

6. Conclusion结论

Final judgment: a useful conditional diagnostic framework. The force-to-carrier construction explains how vortex geometry may move differently from fluid particles. The steady-field negative control and pre-reconnection flux comparison are more persuasive than visual smoothness alone. The method neither restores ideal-fluid physics universally nor tracks a uniquely identifiable line through every reconnection. Use it where its smoothness, null handling and approximation conditions can be checked.

最终判断：值得借鉴的有条件诊断框架。 从力到输运速度的构造解释了涡面几何为何不同于粒子运动。稳态场负对照与重联前通量比较，比视觉平滑性更有说服力。它既不普遍恢复理想流体物理，也不保证每次重联中都能唯一追踪同一涡线。应用前必须检查光滑性、零点处理与近似条件。
